# Principle: Stub Content Lacks Synthesis

**Date**: 2026-03-10
**Source**: Prune cycle 11, Agent 2
**Tags**: pruning, auto-generation, quality

## Pattern

Auto-generated learn stubs with single-paragraph content lack synthesis, pattern extraction, and actionable substance. They are placeholders waiting for deeper elaboration that never comes.

13 lines of YAML frontmatter + 1 sentence = noise, not knowledge.

## Anti-pattern

The oracle_learn auto-ingester creates entries at the moment of discovery, but discovery without reflection produces stubs, not learnings.
